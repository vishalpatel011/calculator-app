Privacy Policy

Calculator for Windows · Effective 31 October 2026

Your data stays on your computer

Everything you enter in Calculator is stored on your own computer, encrypted. The app has no server and does not send your data to the developer or to anyone else.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to connect a Google account, Calculator asks for one permission only: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. With it, the app stores backup copies of its own data in a hidden application folder in your Google Drive.

  • The app cannot see, open, change or delete any of your other files in Google Drive.
  • Backups are encrypted on your computer before they are uploaded. Google cannot read them, and the developer has no access to them.
  • The sign-in permission is stored on your computer, encrypted, and is used only to upload, list and download these backups.

Calculator's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data received from Google is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not transferred to anyone.

No tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

Removing access and deleting backups

  • Disconnect Google Drive at any time from the app's settings.
  • Remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the backups in Google Drive: Settings → Manage apps → Calculator → Options → Delete hidden app data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
